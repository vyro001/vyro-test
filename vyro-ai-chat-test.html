Testing guide

Follow these three steps.

No special setup is needed. Use the chat widget on this page and talk naturally, as though you were arranging a real taxi ride.

01

Start in Chat

Open the Vyro widget and choose Chat. Tell the AI your pickup location, destination, date and time, passenger count, and vehicle preference.

02

Test the AI response

Ask for a fare, change a trip detail, or request a discount. Observe whether the AI updates the details and explains the revised price correctly.

03

Confirm your booking

Provide your own name, valid email address, and phone number. Review the complete summary, explicitly confirm, and check your email for confirmation.

Suggested test cases

See what the assistant can handle.

Try an ordinary booking first, then vary the conversation to check how reliably the AI adapts. For example, schedule a ride between 10 PM and 6 AM to observe how night-fare rules are handled, if applicable to the operator.

  • Request a taxi fare between two locations.
  • Switch between Standard and XL.
  • Choose a late-night pickup time.
  • Ask whether a discount is available.
  • Change your pickup time or destination.
  • Review and explicitly confirm the booking.

Look for your booking confirmation email.

After you confirm the booking through Chat, Vyro should send a confirmation to the email address you provided. Please check your inbox and, if necessary, your spam/junk folder. If no email arrives, report that as part of the test.

Testing note: This widget is connected to the configured Vyro booking system. Confirming a test booking may create a booking record and send notifications. Use an email address and phone number you control, and coordinate with the operator so test reservations are not treated as real ride requests. An internet connection is required for the widget to load.
